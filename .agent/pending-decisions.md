# Pending Decisions and Open Questions

This document compiles features, rules, or implementation details explicitly designated by the user as "undecided," "to be addressed later," or "open questions," together with open questions that come up during development.

It is the only registry of open questions. The spec questions keep their `OQ-xx` IDs (anchor `#oq-xx`), and specs and code refer to them by ID. A decided or closed item stays here with its status.

**Status:** `Open` · `Decided <date>` (decided; implementation or a service confirmation is pending) · `Closed <date>`.
